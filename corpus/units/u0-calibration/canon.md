One placement question, then a short series of math questions sized to your
answer - easy to hard, spanning everything this book leans on: dot products,
matrix shapes, softmax, derivatives, gradient notation, expectations. Nothing
is taught here, and you are expected to run out of sure answers somewhere in
the middle - where that happens is the calibration. Answer fast, mark "I
don't know" freely, move on. The teaching starts next chapter, tuned by what
these answers reveal.
