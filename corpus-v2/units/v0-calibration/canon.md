One placement question, then a short series - easy to hard - spanning what
this book leans on: reading a probability distribution, expectation and
variance, counting combinations, z-scores and what a p-value even is, plus
a few spot checks on ground volume 1 covered (loss, gradients, tokens).
Nothing is taught here, and running out of sure answers partway through is
the intended outcome - where that happens is the calibration. Answer fast,
mark "I don't know" freely, move on. Everything you miss gets re-derived
from first principles exactly where it is needed; these answers only tune
how briefly.
